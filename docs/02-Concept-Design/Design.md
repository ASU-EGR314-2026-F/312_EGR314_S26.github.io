The goal of our project is to create an autonomous robot that can inspect 4inch diameter pipes and report any faults it finds. These faults include debris in the pipe, water leakage, or collapsed sections. The robot needs to be able to survey the straight line conduit reliably and completely autonomously to free up workers to finish other tasks.
Our audience is Sundt construction who has shown interest in this specific design and has highlighted its ideal use cases for us. They have specific parameters they want met such as the pipe diameter, pipe length and expected obstacles. We are also building with the individual workers in mind as we want to make operating the robot as easy as possible and not disrupt their workflow on the jobsite. While Sundt has shown interest in the project its uses are for any other construction or inspection company as well. We are also building the project with the EGR 314 teaching staff in mind as this will also serve as a demonstration of our engineering skills.
