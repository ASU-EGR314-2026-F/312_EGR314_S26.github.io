## Product Mission Statement


 > "Design and build a small diameter pipe inspection device for Sundt Construction to accelerate construction time and improve safety while reducing worker load."

 Our team is committed to improving upon and further developing the autonomous pipe inspection vehicle we developed last semester for Sundt construction. Building on the existing design, we will focus on improving vehicle stability, mobility, reliability, autonomous capabilities, and inspection technology.The vehicle is designed to navigate through small electrical conduits and collect inspection data using onboarding sensors and imaging technology. Through collaboration, engineering, and hands-on problem solving. Our goal is to develop a safe, reliable, and functional vehicle that improves inspection efficiency and reduces construction costs. This project will provide a practical solution for Sundt construction while establishing a strong foundation for future development and continued improvement.
